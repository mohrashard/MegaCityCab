🛠️ Driver Help & Support

1️⃣ Getting Started

Welcome to Mega City Cab! Here's how to get started as a new driver:

1

Sign Up & Login

Enter your credentials to access the driver dashboard. Your username is usually your registered mobile number, and you'll set up a secure password during registration.

Need help with login? Contact support@megacitycab.com

2

Set Up Profile

Complete your profile by adding your vehicle details, contact information, and bank account for payments.

A complete profile helps passengers identify you and ensures smooth payments.

3

Dashboard Overview

Familiarize yourself with the driver dashboard. Here you can track your earnings, view ride history, and check notifications.

The dashboard is your command center for all driver activities.

How to Handle Ride Requests:

  • Receiving a Request: You'll get a notification with all ride details as shown above.
  • Accepting a Ride: Click the 'Accept' button to confirm you'll take the ride.
  • Viewing Details: Review passenger info, pickup/dropoff locations, fare, and payment method.

Tip: Maintaining a high acceptance rate improves your driver rating and increases your chances of getting premium rides!

Step-by-Step Process:

  1. Confirm Pickup: Once you've picked up the passenger, tap "Start Ride" in your app to begin the journey.

  3. Complete the Ride: After reaching the destination, tap "End Ride" to conclude the journey.
  4. Payment Confirmation: Wait for payment confirmation before the passenger exits.

Managing Your Earnings:

  • Checking Earnings: View your total income and a breakdown of rides in the Earnings tab.
  • Admin Commission: A 30% commission is automatically deducted from each ride fare.
  • Withdrawing Funds: Transfer your available balance to your linked bank account by clicking the "Withdraw" button.
  • Processing Time: Withdrawals typically process within 1-2 business days.

Tip: Set a withdrawal schedule to manage your cash flow better - many drivers withdraw weekly or bi-weekly.

5️⃣ Troubleshooting & FAQs

Find solutions to common issues you might encounter:

Why can't I accept rides?

There could be several reasons:

  • Your status might be set to "Offline" - check your availability status
  • Your account might be temporarily suspended - check for notifications
  • You may not have a vehicle assigned - go to Profile > Vehicle Information
  • App might need updating - check for updates in your app store

If issues persist, please contact our support team at support@megacitycab.com or call our 24/7 hotline at 1-800-MEGACAB.

How do I update my bank details?

To update your bank details:

  1. Go to the "Profile" section in the sidebar
  2. Select "Payment Information"
  3. Click "Edit" next to your bank details
  4. Enter your new bank information
  5. Verify with your account password
  6. Submit the changes

Note: It may take 1-2 business days for the verification process to complete.

What happens if a passenger cancels?

Our cancellation policy protects drivers:

  • If a passenger cancels before you arrive, you'll receive a small cancellation fee
  • If you've already arrived at the pickup location, you'll receive a higher cancellation fee
  • If you've waited more than 5 minutes at the pickup location, you're eligible for a full cancellation fee

All cancellation fees are automatically added to your earnings and will appear in your ride history.

How do I report an issue with a passenger?

To report an issue with a passenger:

  1. Go to "Ride History" in the sidebar
  2. Find the specific ride
  3. Click on the "Report Issue" button
  4. Select the issue type from the dropdown
  5. Provide details about what happened
  6. Submit your report

Our support team will review your report and take appropriate action within 24-48 hours.

Why is my payment delayed?

Payment delays can occur for several reasons:

  • The passenger's payment method failed and needs to be updated
  • There's a temporary issue with our payment processing system
  • Your bank account details are incorrect or outdated
  • There's a pending investigation on the ride

If a payment is delayed for more than 48 hours, please contact our support team.
