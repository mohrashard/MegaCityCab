Help & Support – Passenger Guide

1. Booking a Ride 🏁

  • Enter Details: Pickup & Drop Location, Date & Time, Payment Method.
  • Confirm Booking: Submit your request & wait for an assigned driver.
  • Real-time Updates: Get instant notifications on ride status.

2. Managing Your Bookings 📅

  • View Bookings: Check driver details, status, and fare.
  • Modify or Cancel: Change ride details before assignment.
  • Complete Payment: Pay easily via Wallet, Card, or Cash.

3. Wallet & Payments 💳

  • Check Balance: See your wallet balance at a glance.
  • Top-Up Wallet: Add funds using a secure payment gateway.
  • Manage Cards: Add, edit, or delete your saved payment methods.
  • View Transactions: Keep track of your payments & ride history.

4. Contacting Support 📞

  • Need help? Contact your assigned driver from the ride details page.
  • Support Team: Chat with us for booking or payment issues.
  • FAQs Section: Find answers instantly with a search bar.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my ride?
You can cancel your ride by going to "My Bookings" section, selecting the ride you wish to cancel, and clicking the "Cancel" button. Please note that cancellation fees may apply if the driver is already on their way.
How do I add money to my wallet?
To add money to your wallet, navigate to the "Billing" section, click on "Top-Up Wallet," enter the amount you wish to add, select your preferred payment method, and confirm the transaction.
What if my driver doesn't arrive?
If your driver doesn't arrive within the estimated time, you can contact them directly from the ride details page. If you still face issues, use the support chat button to connect with our customer service team for immediate assistance.
